# Contoso refund policy

- Orders can be refunded in full within 30 days of delivery.
- Orders that have not shipped can be cancelled for a full refund at any time.
- Shipped orders that are lost in transit get a free replacement or a full refund, the customer's choice.
- After 30 days, offer store credit instead of a refund.
- Never promise a refund for an order you have not looked up.
